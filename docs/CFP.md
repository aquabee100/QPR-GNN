# Call for papers: IEEE IoT Journal, Special Issue on Graph Representation Learning for Internet of Things

Source: https://ieee-iotj.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Graph_Representation_Learning.pdf (read 2026-10-10)

**Scope.** State-of-the-art research on graph representation learning (GRL),
including GNNs, for IoT: theory, design, implementation and evaluation of GRL
techniques for IoT applications, and their integration with emerging
technologies and next-generation networks. The call notes that GRL-enabled
IoT research is immature and under-explored under practical IoT constraints.

**Topics**
- Lightweight, energy-efficient GRL models for resource-limited devices
- Dynamic GRL algorithms for time-varying network topologies
- Heterogeneous GNNs for multimodal data fusion and device interaction modelling
- Privacy-preserving federated or distributed GRL
- Explainable GRL for reliable decisions in critical IoT scenarios
- GRL-based network optimisation, resource scheduling and overhead reduction
- GRL for IoT security (anomaly detection, threat identification)
- Theoretical analysis and performance benchmarking of GRL in IoT systems
- GRL with RIS, pinching antennas, CFmMIMO and SAGIN for 6G IoT
- GRL-enabled fusion of agentic AI and LLMs for AIoT
